Title: 7 Signs You're Emotionally Burned Out

Here are seven signs you might be
emotionally burned out. And if you
relate to even one, it's time to slow
down. Sign one, you wake up tired even
after sleeping. You slept 8 hours, no
alarms, no interruptions, but you wake
up more tired than when you went to bed.
That kind of exhaustion isn't just
physical, it's emotional. Your body
might be resting, but your mind hasn't
had a real break in a long time. And
when emotional fatigue piles up, even
sleep stops feeling restorative. Sign
two, everything feels overwhelming. When
even the smallest tasks start to feel
impossible, that's a sign burnout might
be taking over. Something as simple as
sending an email could make you feel
like it's a monumental effort. When your
mind starts to treat every task like a
mountain to climb, it's time to pay
attention. Sign three, you've become a
zombie in conversations. You might find
yourself physically present, nodding and
saying the usual polite responses, but
inside your mind has already checked
out. You're not really listening, and
later you can't even remember what was
discussed. This is a sign that your
emotional resources are running on
empty. Sign four, you feel numb instead
of sad or angry. Burnout doesn't always
feel dramatic. Sometimes it's just
nothing. No big emotions, no tears, no
rage, just gray. Like you're stuck in
emotional airplane mode. Sign five. You
start to hate the stuff you used to
love. Remember when you used to enjoy
reading, painting, working out, or just
existing? Now everything feels like a
chore, even things that once made you
feel alive. That's burnout draining the
color out of your life like an emotional
black and white filter. Sign six, you're
extra snappy. You snap at people over
the smallest things, like someone's
chewing too loudly or they ask you a
simple question. It's like your
emotional fuse is so short, anything can
trigger you. Your patience is shot and
everything feels like an attack even
when it's not. Sign seven. You fantasize
about disappearing. You might find
yourself imagining what it would be like
to vanish for a while. Maybe retreat to
the woods or head to the mountains where
there are no responsibilities, no texts,
just peace and quiet. Bonus sign. You
start seeing yourself through a harsh
lens. This one isn't a textbook symptom
of burnout, but it shows up a lot when
you're running on empty. You catch
yourself picking apart your appearance.
You scroll through other people's photos
and feel like you're not just tired,
you're also not enough. That spiral of
comparison and self-criticism doesn't
always mean burnout. But for most
people, it hits hardest when they're
emotionally exhausted. Okay, if you
recognized yourself in any of those
signs, first of all, you're not broken.
You're human and you've been carrying
too much for too long. Burnout doesn't
mean you're weak. It means you've been
strong for too long without enough
support. So, what do you do? Start
small. Sleep more. Say no to things,
even if you feel guilty. Do one tiny
thing that makes you feel something
other than a rock with a to-do list. You
don't have to collapse to deserve
recovery. So now close this video and go
do something gentle with yourself.
